## THE GORGE

Your are trapped deep within a shadowy ravine
With a thin line of sight to the outside world
Will you escape?

Survive the depths and corners
Find hidden secrets
Gather the pieces you need to escape
Will you make it out in time?

Good luck, adventurer
The Gorge awaits your courage and wit

#### CONTROLS

**walk**: `[ w,a,s,d ]` or equivalent

**look around**: `[ mouse ]` or equivalent

**sneak**: `[ shift ]` or equivalent

**crawl**: `[ shift + s + look_down ]` or equivalent

**interact**: `[ right click ]` or equivalent

**attack/push**: `[ left click ]` or equivalent

**jump**: `[ space ]` or equivalent
